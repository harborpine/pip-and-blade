# Pip & Blade: update brief 3 ("clean and flat")

Read `BRIEF.md`, `BRIEF-2.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/design.md`, `docs/redesign.md`,
`docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/PROGRESS.md` first. This brief adds to them. Where it disagrees
with an earlier brief about how the game LOOKS, this one wins. How the game PLAYS does not
change at all.

## Why

The owner played version 0.2.0 on their iPhone and says it is "way better", and the new
dice controls work. But the dice are still not clean to look at, and they now want the whole
game in a simpler, cleaner style: one that is easy to produce well and consistently.

They were shown options and chose:

1. **Dice: "ivory dice".** A flat, front-on die: a cream rounded square with the hero's
   colour on the rim, the action's icon in that colour on the left, and a dark number on
   the right. No tilted 3D cube.
2. **The whole game in that same flat style**, including the characters.
3. **Characters: round tokens.** Every hero, enemy and boss becomes a coin with a simple
   face on it, like chips in a board game. Not full-body figures.
4. **Lettering: the iPhone's own clean rounded text everywhere.** The blocky pixel lettering
   goes.

Make every other design decision yourself, record the big ones in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and
do not stop to ask questions. The owner is not a programmer.

## The style, in one paragraph

Flat, calm and crisp. Solid colours, no shading, no bevels, no textures, no pixel art, no
outlines thicker than they need to be. Rounded corners. A small shared palette. Everything
is drawn as smooth shapes that stay sharp at any size. If a thing can be drawn with fewer
shapes, draw it with fewer shapes. When in doubt, remove detail.

## What to build

### Dice (the most looked-at thing in the game)

- A rounded square, cream ("ivory") face, hero-colour rim. At least 72px on screen, at least
  64px on the smallest phones.
- Left: the action's icon, solid, in the hero's colour. Right: the value as a large dark
  number. Both must be readable at arm's length.
- Small quiet marks for the extras, never competing with the icon and number: the die's
  set symbol as a small mark in a corner, and keyword marks (Cleave, Pierce and so on) as a
  row of small icons along the bottom edge.
- States, each obvious at a glance and never relying on colour alone:
  kept (gold ring, and the padlock under it shown closed), used (greyed and flat), blank
  (cream face with a grey dash, no number), cursed (purple tint with a crack mark, no
  number), jammed (a chain mark, padlock replaced by a chain), lifted (raised with a soft
  ring while choosing a target).
- The padlock under each die stays, drawn in the same flat style, at least 44px.

### Characters as round tokens

- Hero: cream coin, rim in the hero's colour. Enemy: dark coin, grey rim. Elite enemy:
  dark coin, red rim. Boss: a clearly larger coin (about 1.5 times) with a gold rim.
- Inside each coin, a simple face or emblem built from a few flat shapes that captures who
  it is (the knight's helmet and plume, the duelist's hat and feather, the rat's ears and
  teeth, the bell's crack and eye). All 36 must be told apart at a glance at about 64px,
  by shape as well as colour.
- A downed hero is a greyed coin. Tokens still hop, shake and flash as before.
- Use the names and one-line looks already in `src/shared/roster.ts` as the brief for each.

### Icons

- One consistent family of solid, flat, single-colour icons on a shared grid: same corner
  rounding, same weight, same visual size. Covers every icon in the game: action effects,
  set symbols, keywords, statuses, enemy intentions, doors, set badges, interface icons and
  all 40 items.
- Meaning first: a sword is unmistakably a sword at 20px. One object per icon.

### Interface

- Buttons, panels, sheets, chips, health bars, tabs and switches are plain flat shapes drawn
  by the app itself (rounded rectangles in the shared palette), not picture files. Cream
  buttons with dark text, one gold primary button per screen, red only for danger.
- The dice tray is a flat dark panel. Enemy intentions stay as speech bubbles with an icon,
  a number and a short word label.
- Backgrounds: one calm flat backdrop per act (two or three large soft shapes that hint at
  forest, flooded vault, volcanic spire) and one for menus. Nothing busy behind the play
  area.
- Text: the phone's rounded system lettering throughout, heavy for titles and numbers.
  Nothing smaller than 12px. Remove the pixel lettering completely.
- App icon and home-screen icons redrawn to match: an ivory die on a calm colour field.

### How the art is made (keep the original promise)

- All graphics are still generated by code with one command (`npm run art`), with no
  downloaded or hand-drawn files and no AI image services. They are now smooth vector
  drawings rather than pixel pictures, so they stay sharp at every size.
- The old 3D "voxel" generator and its pixel pictures are retired. The automatic content
  check must be updated to match the new truth: every picture on the asset list is a flat
  vector drawing, none is a leftover pixel picture, and none is missing. This replaces the
  old "every icon is a 3D toy" check because the owner changed the requirement; it is not
  a loosening.
- The gallery page stays and shows every picture with its name.

## What must not change or break

- **How the game plays.** Tap a die to use it, a padlock under each die, Reroll, Undo, End
  Turn, long-press to peek, exact previews and forecasts, the tutorial steps: all exactly as
  in 0.2.0. No game rules or numbers change; the balance results must be identical.
- **Scrolling.** Every sheet and list still scrolls by finger; the page itself never does.
- **The owner's saved progress.** Saves made by 0.1.0 and by 0.2.0 must both load and
  resume. Add a 0.2.0 save to `tests/fixtures/` before changing anything.
- **Every existing test and threshold.** Tests that describe the old look (for example
  "every icon is a voxel toy") are replaced by the equivalent test for the new look and the
  replacement is listed in the final report. Nothing else is weakened, skipped or deleted.
- Works in a Safari tab and installed to the home screen, offline, no audio, no network
  calls, portrait, safe areas, tap targets at least 44px, main controls in thumb reach,
  reduce motion honoured, no animation running while idle.
- Leave the separate "what-to-watch" project completely alone.

## Agent team

Create a real agent team as before. At most 3 teammates working at once; shut each down when
its work is done. The lead coordinates, reviews and integrates and does not do teammates'
work. Folder ownership is unchanged (see `CLAUDE.md`).

- **lead (Opus)**: writes `docs/flat-style.md` BEFORE spawning anyone: the exact palette,
  the die anatomy with sizes, the token rules, the icon grid and weight, the surface styles,
  the asset list changes and the new content check. Makes the 0.2.0 save fixture. Reviews
  the style sheet and every screen. Runs `npm run verify`. Publishes.
- **art (Opus)**: the new generator, every icon, all 36 tokens, backgrounds, app icons,
  gallery. Style sheet first (see below). Looks at its own output at phone size before
  calling anything done.
- **ui (Opus)**: applies the style to every screen, draws the surfaces, removes the pixel
  lettering and picture-based frames, keeps every test id and behaviour, keeps
  `src/ui/TESTING.md` exact.
- **qa (Sonnet)**: keeps the whole suite green without loosening it, replaces look-specific
  checks with their new equivalents, adds the 0.2.0 save test, retakes and reviews
  screenshots of every screen, reports defects to the owners.
- **engine (Opus)** and **designer (Opus)**: not expected to be needed.

Lessons from the last update, to apply this time:

- A teammate that does a very large job in one go can run out of room. Teammates commit a
  working checkpoint after every piece, and the lead replaces a teammate that stalls with a
  fresh one and a written handover rather than waiting.
- Teammates run the tester's layout checks themselves against a private copy while they
  work, instead of passing fixes back and forth.
- Only one teammate at a time builds into the shared `dist/` folder or runs the full suite.
- The owner's plan has a usage limit. If work is cut off, nothing saved is lost; resume from
  `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

### Order of work

1. lead: `docs/flat-style.md`, the 0.2.0 save fixture, asset list and content check changes.
2. art: a **style sheet first**: three dice in every state, about twelve icons, six tokens
   (two heroes, two enemies, one elite, one boss), one backdrop, composed into a mock fight
   screen at the owner's phone size. The lead reviews it against this brief, sends the mock
   to the owner for information (without waiting for a reply), and only then is the full
   set produced. ui starts on the surfaces and lettering in parallel.
3. art produces everything; ui applies it screen by screen; qa keeps the suite green and
   retakes screenshots.
4. lead and qa review every screen until nothing from the old look remains and nothing looks
   cluttered. Then the full check, publish, confirm live.
